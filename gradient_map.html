Read the vertical scale carefully: it is d(soma peak)/d log(local metric scale), in mV. These are small local effects. The strongest diameter direction predicts about −0.000974 mV for a 1% change on a 2.356 mV peak. This is a sensitivity map, not evidence that dendrites literally perform gradient descent.
The spatial pattern is the interesting part. Before the first synaptic site, local length has a tiny positive tangent. At the compartment containing the first site it flips negative; downstream it stays negative. Diameter is negative across the entire branch and is strongest immediately proximal to the first site. This is compatible with competing cable effects—added membrane/load versus axial coupling—but the map itself does not assign a biological optimization objective.
